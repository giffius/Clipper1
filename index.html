80 Years of Excellence

Simmons Lock & Key has been proudly serving the Grand Valley since 1945. What started on Main Street in downtown Grand Junction has grown into the region's most trusted locksmith service.

We're a family-owned business that treats our customers like family. We pride ourselves on one-on-one professional customer service with hometown, small-town respect and values.

Whether you need a simple key duplication or a complete master system for a commercial property, we're here to help with the same dedication and expertise that has served Grand Junction for eight decades.
